BUILD YOUR FIRST PC

第一次自己組電腦

作者：Smile Corner｜節錄原稿第 2–3 頁

From “I Want a Computer” to “I Built It”

從「我想要一台電腦」到「這台是我自己組的」

This book follows one simple learning path: first decide what the computer needs to do, then choose compatible parts, assemble them carefully, and finally complete the first successful boot.

這本書只走一條清楚的學習路線：先決定電腦要做什麼，再挑選彼此相容的零件，小心完成組裝，最後成功第一次開機。

Know what you need. Choose the right parts. Build safely. Boot successfully.

第一冊目標：知道需求、選對零件、安全組裝、成功開機。

You do not need to memorize every specification. The goal is to understand enough to make sensible decisions and know where to check when something does not fit.

你不需要背下所有規格。真正的目標，是理解到足以做出合理選擇，也知道零件不相容時該從哪裡檢查。

Safety Comes Before Speed

安全永遠比速度重要

Before building, prepare a clean, dry work area. Turn devices off before changing internal hardware. Disconnect external power before installing or removing components.

開始組裝前，先準備乾淨、乾燥的工作空間。更換內部硬體前先關機，安裝或拆除零件以前要先拔除外部電源。

Work slowly and read the motherboard and case manuals. Do not force a connector that does not fit naturally. Keep screws organized so none are left loose inside the case. Avoid touching exposed contacts unnecessarily. Do not open a power supply unit itself as a beginner project.

慢慢做、先看說明書；接頭不對就不要硬插；螺絲要整理好；盡量不要摸金屬接點；初學者不要拆開電源供應器本體。

Rule: If you are not sure, stop and check first.

規則：不確定時，先停下來確認。

HOME SERVER & NAS FOR BEGINNERS

家庭伺服器與 NAS 入門

作者：Smile Corner｜節錄原稿第 3–4 頁

1. What Is a Home Server?

什麼是家庭伺服器？

A home server is a computer that provides services to other devices on your network, such as files, backups, or media.

家庭伺服器是在家中網路提供檔案、備份或影音等服務的電腦。

What would you check first, and why?

你會先檢查哪一項？為什麼？

2. What Is NAS?

什麼是 NAS？

Network-attached storage focuses on shared storage over the network. It can be a dedicated appliance or a computer configured for the job.

NAS 主要提供網路共享儲存，可以是專用設備，也可以是設定好的電腦。
