Poisonous Plants of North America & Taiwan

A Visual Safety Guide to Dangerous Wild Plants, Toxic Berries, Look-Alikes, and Plants You Should Never Eat

這本書的目的不是教讀者冒險採食，而是幫助一般人先認出風險：漂亮的花、鮮豔的果實、熟悉的葉形，都不代表安全。書中涵蓋北美與台灣常見高風險植物、相似植物誤認、接觸型危害，以及遇到不確定植物時應該先停止、查證的基本安全原則。

Core rule

Never taste an unknown plant to identify it.

Beautiful does not mean edible. Color is not a toxicity test. Cooking is not a universal antidote.
